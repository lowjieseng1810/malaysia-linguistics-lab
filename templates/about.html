About the Project

A digital space for exploring Malaysia's living language heritage.

Malaysia Linguistics Lab — Exploring Malaysia's Languages Through Technology is an educational project designed to make selected Malaysian languages, communities, and cultural contexts easier to discover through interactive learning.

4 languages 12 learning levels Interactive cultural exploration
01
The Purpose

Why this project exists

Discover

Make languages easier to encounter

Many of Malaysia's languages can be difficult to discover through ordinary learning platforms. This project brings selected languages into one accessible digital experience.

Learn

Begin with practical expressions

Short learning levels introduce useful words and expressions through guided steps, questions, hints, and saved progress.

Connect

Place language in cultural context

Maps, visual records, and contextual information help connect language learning with the people, places, and living heritage around it.
